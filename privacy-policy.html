Privacy policy for the Aotearoa Species Classifier app for Android and iOS devices

Last updated: 27 July 2025 The Aotearoa Species Classifier app for Android and iOS, available from Google Play and Apple's app store, respectively, requires access to the camera of the device that runs the app so that photos can be taken. The app stores and processes these photos locally on the device, using local inference with the neural network model included in the app, and does not send them anywhere. The web application available at https://what-is-this.cms.waikato.ac.nz temporarily stores uploaded images so that they can be processed by the neural network model running on the server that hosts the web application. If you have any questions regarding the app, contact eibe@waikato.ac.nz.
